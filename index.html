05 - Week 1 Analysis

What went right, what went wrong

Week 1 gave useful early signals, but it should not be treated as the final result of the campaign.

What went well

  • 54 clicks from 104 opens means more than half of openers clicked through: 51.92% click-to-open.
  • Ian and Kristi led volume with 16 clicks each, together driving 32 of 54 total clicks.
  • Desmond English had the highest open rate: 15 opens from 23 delivered, or 65.22%.
  • Pia Mining was small but very warm: 3 opens and 3 clicks, a 100.00% click-to-open rate.
  • No spam complaints and no soft bounces appeared anywhere in the Week 1 GHL export.

What went wrong

  • 34 hard bounces is high for the first week: 11.81% of 288 send attempts.
  • Desmond's two lists caused 26 of 34 hard bounces, or 76.47% of all hard bounces.
  • Desmond French got 18 opens but 0 clicks. That points to a CTA, link, translation, or offer-matching problem to check before resending.
  • David Oletu underperformed on engagement: 13 opens from 49 delivered and only 3 clicks.
  • Pia Non-Mining had 11 skipped contacts, which is 22.00% of the 50 visible contacts in that segment.
  • 0 conversions and $0 tracked revenue means interest has not yet turned into donations inside the platform data.

Week 2 moves

  1. Clean Desmond's email lists first, especially the English list with 17 hard bounces from 40 attempts.
  2. Check every French CTA and Givebutter link before the next send because 18 opens produced 0 clicks.
  3. Use Ian and Kristi as the engagement model: both had 45.83% opens, 33.33% click rates, and 72.73% click-to-open.
  4. Fix skipped-contact rules for Pia Non-Mining so the 11 skipped contacts are not lost again in Week 2.
  5. Add a clearer donation step or follow-up ask, because the campaign has attention but 0 tracked conversions so far.

Plain-English takeaway

Week 1 did not fail. It proved that the audience will open and click when the sender/list fit is strong. The next improvement is practical: clean bounced addresses, repair skipped-contact issues, tighten the French CTA, and turn the strongest click behavior into a clearer Givebutter donation path.
