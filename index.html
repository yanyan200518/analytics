What Went Right And What Needs Fixing

This is the analyst read. The campaign is getting attention, but the list and donation path need work.

Area What went right What needs fixing Next move
Reach Friday delivered 607, up 353 from Week 1. The list is still small compared with larger campaigns, so the ceiling for donations is limited. Build more verified contacts, not just more contacts.
Attention Friday open rate reached 59.47%, the best of the three report lines. Opens alone do not equal donations. Use the same strength in subject line and sender trust, then make the next action clearer.
Clicks Friday had 132 summary clicks and 1,214 total link clicks. Clicks were spread across donation, YouTube, website, and Instagram. The donation path was not dominant enough. Make Givebutter the primary link. Keep supporting links lower in the email.
Donation result People are engaging with the story and links. GHL shows $0 revenue and 0 conversions so far. Check the donation page, the CTA wording, tracking setup, and whether the email ask is specific enough.
List quality Hard bounce rate improved from 11.81% in Week 1 to 5.30% on Friday. 5.30% is still too high and can hurt sender reputation. Remove hard bounces, verify new emails before upload, and use email deliverability checks such as Mailgun or a verifier before sending.
Unsubscribes Spam complaints stayed at 0. Friday unsubscribed count was 7, or 1.15%, which is high against benchmark. Segment more carefully. Avoid sending to weak fit contacts just to increase volume.
Tracking GHL shows the number of clicks by link, so we can see which links pulled attention. From this report view, we should not say that a specific person clicked a specific link. Use GHL contact level exports where available, UTM naming, and Bitly or link checkers before sending follow ups.

Best read from Friday

The email is not the main problem. The audience opened and clicked. The bigger issue is converting attention into donations and cleaning the email list so future sends protect sender reputation.

Follow up direction

Write the follow up around one clear giving action, one emotional reason to act now, and one visible Givebutter button. Keep proof links, but do not let them compete with the donation link.
